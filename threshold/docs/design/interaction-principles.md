# Interaction Principles

- One meaningful action and one clear contextual cue at a time.
- Reveal detail through sheets and focused views.
- Use motion only to explain spatial continuity or state change.
- Preserve the map context when a marker/place is selected.
- Avoid gamification, decorative motion, and feed-like urgency.
- Visual subtlety must not weaken screen-reader semantics, contrast, readable sizing, or touch targets.
