# THE THRESHOLD — Product Requirements

## Definition

**THE THRESHOLD** is “a place-curation service that rediscovers hidden places through human eyes.” It is a digital place of hospitality where people share the context they noticed and users recover their own judgment.

## Problem

- Ratings, ads, CTR, and popularity replace individual judgment.
- Administrative geography fragments atmosphere, proportion, and lived context.
- Technical defaults can dilute the product and design thesis.

## Audience

Urban walkers fatigued by uniform algorithmic recommendations who seek personal criteria and emotional richness.

## Phase 1 hypothesis

Human curation and contextual language can change how a person discovers and chooses a place.

## Phase 1 scope

- A quiet subjective map with the user's position and curated place markers.
- Contextual place cards and place detail.
- Seuil metadata remains available in Passage detail; the main-screen slider is not part of the current interface.
- Save and personal curation foundations.
- Walk mode: start, record observations, finish, and review a trace.
- Handoff to Naver Map or KakaoMap for navigation.

## Out of scope for Phase 1

- Ratings, popularity sorting, and follower-count ranking.
- Personalized AI recommendations, embeddings, Pinecone, and vector search.
- A generic search-first experience.
- Paid map rendering, geocoding, or distance APIs.

## Success signals

Context engagement → meaningful place view → save → curation → return discovery. See `docs/analytics/north-star.md`.

## Vision

Build timeless digital heritage whose value accumulates through human observation.
