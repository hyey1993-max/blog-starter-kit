# THE THRESHOLD · 커리어의 지도

## Project

The career edition of THE THRESHOLD. Same thesis as the city edition: human observation over algorithmic authority. Here the terrain is a career. It shows wandering honestly instead of declaring a destination, and it is grounded in the 돌아보는 길 reflection (seven questions, scripture layer removed).

## Source of truth

- Shared principles, visual and interaction rules: `../threshold/AGENTS.md` and `../threshold/docs/`
- This edition: `docs/product/PRD.md`, `docs/product/ubiquitous-language.md`, `docs/product/user-flows.md`, `docs/analytics/event-taxonomy.md`

## Edition-specific rules

1. The Flâneur's position is a fog, never a pin, score, or "best-fit job".
2. No job recommendations, salary or title ranking, match percentages, or personality types.
3. Ordering is explainable: shared Threads with the Flâneur's own reflection, then editorial order.
4. Passages are moments written by the people who lived them. AI must not write or embellish them.
5. "Still wandering" is shown as a valid state, never as a problem to fix.
6. Reflection answers stay on-device; analytics never carry answer text or stations.

Development rules and the completion bar follow `../threshold/AGENTS.md`.
